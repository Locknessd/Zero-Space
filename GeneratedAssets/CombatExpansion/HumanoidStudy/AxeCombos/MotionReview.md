# Axe combo motion review

Reviewed all 33 GUID/localId identities in `Study.json` using their Mankey two-view sheets and 60 Hz trajectories. I compared the Pepe retarget sheets for representative short, root-travel, and long sequences; the timing and gross actions are retained, while the green study silhouette makes hand-to-grip placement hard to judge on Pepe. The TSV carries one row per source identity, with candidate beats rounded to visible trajectory landmarks and sheet poses. Beat times describe likely strike-shaped extrema, not confirmed contact events; where several poses could be windup, strike, or recovery, the row says so.

Many clips visibly use both axes. In sampled poses, the props do not consistently sit cleanly in the palms, and cross-body poses can put a blade close to the torso or the far hand. Treat grip/weapon alignment as a retarget adaptation item for both avatars, especially for Pepe where the green silhouette obscures the fingers. High-speed frames also make blade direction ambiguous in the two-view sheets. Root-motion variants travel forward through the sampled hips (`hipsEnd.z` in Study.json); their `Inplace` siblings remain near the starting position. The `Combo_03` family stays near place despite its large duration. Several numbered clip/cut variants repeat a broader combo motif but have distinct source identities and timing; they are not interchangeable based on name alone.

This is a source-motion read only. Neither the sheets nor trajectories show a victim, so no entry establishes hit contact, damage timing, gameplay suitability, or integrated animation quality. No clip is marked unusable solely from this isolated study.
