# KB_Hits reaction motion review

Reviewed all 37 distinct `guid:localId` identities in `Study.json`, using each corrected Mankey and Pepe two-view sheet and the matching 60 Hz trajectory. The two fighters show the same broad response families, with scale and pose-fit differences; labels such as high/mid/low and front/side/back describe the source variant and are not proof of where a paired attack would contact.

The weak variants show a short brace/recoil and return upright without root travel. Medium variants fold or crouch more deeply, then rise; their extracted root trajectories translate roughly 0.29–0.99 m, usually along local negative Z, with some lateral component. Stagger variants show balance loss, a fall, and a late rise, with roughly 0.90–1.51 m root travel. Back-hit trajectories move in the opposite Z direction. These are useful candidate families for light hit, medium hit, and knockdown stagger respectively, subject to pairing and blend review.

`BindPose` (7400000) and `tpose` (7400002) are static one-frame support poses, not reactions. `KB_HitOnGroundBack` (7400070) and `KB_HitOnGroundFront` (7400072) begin already prone and hold the downed pose; they may support a separately validated downed state, but show neither impact nor recovery. The medium `KB_Hit_m_MidTop_Med` (7400054) looks close to `KB_Hit_m_MidFront_Med` (7400044); retain as a possible near-duplicate because the sampled poses are not pixel-identical and the distinction is subtle.

The TSV records visual response and root end displacement for every stable identity. Direction and severity are observational classifications from isolated motion sheets, while the named body region remains a source label. These studies cannot establish contact point, attacker compatibility, collision timing, gameplay feel, or integrated quality; root and end-state behavior must be checked in paired runtime use.
