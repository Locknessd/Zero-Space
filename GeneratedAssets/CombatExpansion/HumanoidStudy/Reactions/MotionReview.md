# KB_Hits reaction motion review

Reviewed all 37 distinct `guid:localId` identities in `Study.json`, using each corrected Mankey and Pepe two-view sheet and the matching 60 Hz trajectory. The two fighters show the same broad response families, with scale and pose-fit differences; labels such as high/mid/low and front/side/back describe the source variant and are not proof of where a paired attack would contact.

The weak variants show a short brace/recoil and return upright without root travel. Medium variants fold or crouch more deeply, then rise; their extracted root trajectories translate roughly 0.29–0.99 m, usually along local negative Z, with some lateral component. Stagger variants show strong recoil, a deep torso fold or crouch, then recovery to a standing guard in both fighters. None reaches the ground or holds a downed pose. Their root-motion trajectories travel roughly 0.90–1.51 m; back-hit trajectories move in the opposite Z direction. Classify these as severe standing recoil/stumble candidates, not knockdowns; they are unsuitable alone as lethal/downed states. Pairing and blend behavior remain unreviewed.

`BindPose` (7400000) and `tpose` (7400002) are static one-frame support poses, not reactions. `KB_HitOnGroundBack` (7400070) and `KB_HitOnGroundFront` (7400072) begin already prone and hold the downed pose; they may support a separately validated downed state, but show neither impact nor recovery. The medium `KB_Hit_m_MidTop_Med` (7400054) looks close to `KB_Hit_m_MidFront_Med` (7400044); retain as a possible near-duplicate because the sampled poses are not pixel-identical and the distinction is subtle.

The TSV records visual response and root end displacement for every stable identity. All five Stagger identities (7400060 HighBack, 7400062 HighFront, 7400064 MidFront, 7400066 MidLeft, 7400068 MidRight) were checked on both Mankey and Pepe sheets against their matching trajectories: pelvis height stays well above the floor, feet remain supporting the body, and the late frames return to standing. Direction and severity are observational classifications from isolated motion sheets, while the named body region remains a source label. These studies cannot establish contact point, attacker compatibility, collision timing, gameplay feel, or integrated quality; root and end-state behavior must be checked in paired runtime use.
