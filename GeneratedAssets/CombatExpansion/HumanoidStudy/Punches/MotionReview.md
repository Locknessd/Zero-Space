# Punch motion review

Reviewed 51 unique `(GUID, localID)` identities from `Study.json`; both Mankey and Pepe CPU-skinned sheets were inspected. The TSV has exactly one row per identity, including the two support poses.

Candidate times come from 60 Hz hand motion relative to chest/hips, with the nearest labeled Mankey sheet frame cited in each row. They are preliminary extension/strike candidates, never confirmed contacts. Root translation is reported separately from foot stepping and torso motion.

All 49 non-support clips are provisionally usable; no duplicate candidates were assigned because apparent variants show distinct visible sequences or timing/trajectory differences.

Pepe retargets preserve the broad action sequences. Its narrower silhouette makes the distal hand less legible in oblique poses; rotational elbow and backfist clips spend appreciable time rear-facing and need orientation/spacing calibration. No obvious malformed poses were seen in inspected sheets.

Open calibration items: victim spacing, contact surface/pose, reaction choice, and contact timing remain unverified until paired in Unity. Long turn clips need particular attention to facing and recovery.

The TSV separates source and fighter observations and records exact source identity, duration, status, visible sequence, candidate beats, stance/root travel, and limitations.
