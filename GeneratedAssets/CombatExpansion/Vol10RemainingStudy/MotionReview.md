Times below combine the visible poses and 60 Hz receiver trajectories, not uniformly distributed hit events. There are no independent punch contacts in these nine sequences. Capture is an interaction cue; controlled lifts and turns retain the authored receiver performance. GSWING has an initial takedown and a later thrown landing; the intervening rotations are not extra damage contacts. Later settling bounces in the other clips are not separately awarded damage.

The first uncorrected studies expose floor penetration on strongly inverted JPBOM and GS1 poses, especially on the large-headed Mankey. BRAIN cannot return directly to standing idle. These findings require saved adaptation and Play Mode validation before final approval. Source study sheets and preliminary timings alone do not establish grounded mesh contact, clean recovery, authoritative health or presentation completeness.
