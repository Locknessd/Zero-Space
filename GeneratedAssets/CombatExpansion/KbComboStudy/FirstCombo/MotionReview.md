# First KB candidate visual review

Reviewed both avatars on the positive lane at 0.65 m, 0.80 m and 0.95 m using
the saved timestamped side/oblique sheets. These are preview captures with a
neutral floor; they are not BattleScene gameplay-camera approval.

The left jab, right jab and right hook are distinct readable attacks. The
0.65 m pair is crowded. At 0.80 m and 0.95 m the straight punches visibly
approach the face/head region; the provisional mid-front victim reaction
folds the receiver downward. That reaction selection needs reassessment
against the actual surface-contact measurements. The filename-based body-jab
assumption must not become the final reaction mapping.

The hook has a separate chamber and later cross-body strike. Its contact
must be measured against the receiver after the preceding reactions; the
source extension timestamp alone does not prove a hit. Larger spacing also
makes the hook reach more doubtful in the Mankey view.

Native equivalence and reverse-seek checks passed in the original 12-case
capture, but none of these candidates has accepted contacts or a gameplay
registration. The new FirstComboContacts study measures head and axial torso
surfaces independently before choosing reaction variants, spacing or timings.
