# Camera reference — Cinematic Attack Animation

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y8dA1CIABAk
Author: Rizky Sanjaya. Reference duration: about 9 seconds.
Local inspection: downloaded reference video plus overview/middle/end contact sheets.

Observed composition and movement:

- 0–1 s: elevated three-quarter full shot, with room around the sword and a readable anticipation pose.
- 1–3.25 s: the frame follows the airborne body, changes its viewing direction and moves closer.
- 3.5–4.5 s: the framing shifts around the downward/diagonal attack, leaving room for the large cyan arc.
- 4.5–6.25 s: the camera follows through toward the ground and lowers its viewing angle for the finish.
- 6.5–8.75 s: a steadier, wider recovery view holds the character and the aftermath in frame.

These are visual observations, not measured source camera transforms.
The BOM shot plan adapts this rhythm to its own five attack clips; it does not copy the reference footage/audio.
The existing 17-second BOM introduction and the final fixed blink composition are retained.
Earlier showcase scenes keep their existing camera behavior.
